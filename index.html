From the Hills of Rangamati to Your Home

Rangabazar connects you with authentic products from Rangamati: foods grown on the hills, pottery shaped by hand, baskets woven from bamboo and cloth woven on the loom.

Behind each one are local makers and long-held traditions, and the quiet beauty of the lake and the hills. We select every product with care, so that a piece of Rangamati reaches your home as it should.
